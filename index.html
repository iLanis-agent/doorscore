DoorScore

By the eighth viewing, "the one with the nice kitchen" is all you remember. DoorScore turns every visit into a weighted score the same day - before the blur sets in.

Score a viewing

Set your criteria and their weights - price, commute, light, whatever matters to you. After each viewing, rate it 1-10 on each. The leaderboard ranks them all, and any apartment that scores 3 or less on a single criterion gets flagged as a deal-breaker no matter how good the average looks. Everything stays in your browser.
